Applicant Profile

Fill this in once. The extension reads it automatically on every page of the application (personal info, activities, awards, essays — whatever's on screen).

Resume (activities, awards, experience, essay material — anything else)

Paste in your resume, or write freeform. Extracurriculars, honors/awards, work and volunteer experience, leadership, essay drafts — anything that doesn't fit a fixed field above goes here. The extension reads this as context for every field it can't answer from the structured info, and will automatically fold in new facts you enter later (kept in sync from the extension's side panel).

Previous Writing / Essays (style reference)

Paste in past essays, personal statements, or other writing you're proud of and want the extension to sound like. This is used purely to calibrate voice and tone — vocabulary, sentence rhythm, how reflective vs. matter-of-fact you write — when the extension drafts new essay answers. It's not treated as a source of facts the way the resume above is, so it's fine to include drafts about topics unrelated to this application.
